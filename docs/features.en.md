# Features

The detail of what QueueCare does, moved out of the [README](../README.en.md) so it can open with what matters. (Portuguese version: [funcionalidades.md](funcionalidades.md).)

## What it does
- **Pet owners** register pets (size, coat, weight, temperament, health notes, vaccines) and book by service, pet, day and slot; the **duration and price follow the pet's size and coat**, and the app jumps to the first day with a free slot. They see **until when they can cancel or reschedule for free** before confirming and, after that, message the studio on WhatsApp. They follow the visit (arrived, in progress, ready for pickup), see each pet's history and join a **waiting list** when a day is full.
- **Studios** (owner and attendants) run the day and week schedule and the queue, see the pet's file in front of whoever attends (with a behavior warning), open WhatsApp with a ready-made message (pet ready, **next-day reminder**, waiting-list call), record **no-shows** with a per-owner counter, and configure services (price and duration by size and coat), working hours, team, a public page and the cancellation window.
- **Administrators** create and manage accounts, with no access to schedules or business data.
- No online payment and no automatic messaging (zero-cost constraint): WhatsApp opens with the message ready and staff send it. A manual Pix deposit is proposed in ADR 0023.

### Ready for real use (ADRs 0024 to 0033)
| Area | What exists |
|---|---|
| **Operations** | [Time off and holidays](adr/0024-folgas-feriados-e-ferias.md) (owners cannot book a closed day); [Clients](adr/0025-clientes-do-estudio-e-senha-temporaria.md) (counter search and a temporary password generated by the studio owner, no e-mail needed); [counter registration and booking](adr/0033-cadastro-e-reserva-pelo-balcao.md) (staff book for customers who call or message on WhatsApp); [undo the last attendance step](adr/0026-desfazer-etapa-do-atendimento.md); [studio summary](adr/0031-resumo-do-estudio.md) (attendances, honestly named amounts, no-shows and lapsed clients) |
| **Trust and data** | [Privacy policy, download your data, delete your account by anonymization](adr/0027-lgpd-minima.md) |
| **Recovery** | [Encrypted backup with a restore drill on **every PR**](adr/0028-backup-e-restauracao.md): CI restores into a second PostgreSQL and requires the result to be identical |
| **Operations and support** | [A request id on every response and every log line](adr/0029-identificador-de-requisicao-e-operacao.md), `ops/smoke.sh`, [a warm API during opening hours and a down alert](adr/0032-api-acordada-e-alerta.md), and a [deploy, rollback and incident runbook](operacao.md) |
| **Mobile** | [Installable app that opens offline](adr/0030-app-instalavel-e-offline.md) (the service worker never stores API data) |

Every decision has an ADR with what was **rejected** and the **known risks**, including the limits of the zero-cost constraint.
